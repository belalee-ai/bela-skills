# Riso 孔版叠印拼贴

Internal ID: `risograph-collage`

## North star

Recompose the source from two or three spot-color layers, coarse halftone, cropped photo fragments, and visibly imperfect registration. Let ink interaction create the image; do not fake a neon filter.

## Best for

Street scenes, portraits, events, architecture, signs, crowds with one clear focal group, repeated objects, and frames with strong light-dark separation.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 彩色立面、门窗重复、街铺和招棚适合分成套色层。
- `风景：中` — 天空、地面和植物颜色分区清楚时可用，柔雾风景不适合。
- `人像：强` — 主体够大、动作或服装色彩鲜明时最能发挥粗网点与错版。

## Preserve

- One hero or focal group.
- One recognizable crop or silhouette.
- One repeated source structure that can carry a halftone or overprint.

## Delete and transform

- Remove natural-color fidelity and most small details.
- Split the image into two or three spot separations with one deliberate overlap color.
- Use one photographic fragment and one flat silhouette family; keep registration drift subtle but visible.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium`.
- Keep the hero large enough to survive coarse screening.
- Use one strong crop and one overprint collision; avoid a tile collage.

## Color and material

Choose two or three from fluorescent coral, soy blue, violet, yellow, forest, and black; uncoated paper, dot gain, screen grain, dry ink, and 1–3 mm apparent misregistration.

## Avoid

Full CMYK realism, rainbow palettes, glitch effects, vaporwave glow, random halftone on every surface, fake magazine text, extra stickers, logos, watermark, and perfectly aligned digital edges.

## Quality gate

The spot layers must interact through overprint and misregistration. If the result is merely duotone photography, the style failed.
