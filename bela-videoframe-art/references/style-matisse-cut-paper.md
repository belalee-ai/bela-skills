# 马蒂斯式剪纸色域

Internal ID: `matisse-cut-paper`

## North star

Translate the source into a few scissor-cut organic color shapes with lively figure-ground play. Borrow the physical logic of painted cut paper without copying a known artwork, motif, or exact palette.

## Best for

Clear bodies, dance, plants, animals, gestures, bright objects, and scenes whose action remains legible through silhouette and spacing.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：弱` — 只有拱门、楼梯或大块立面能变成有机剪纸形时才适合。
- `风景：中` — 海岸、山坡、树木和植物具有大面积正负形时可用。
- `人像：强` — 舞蹈、运动、伸展和完整身体动作最适合；细腻脸部肖像不适合。

## Preserve

- The hero's pose, direction, and one relationship cue.
- Up to three distinctive silhouette anchors.
- One source-derived negative shape between subjects or limbs.

## Delete and transform

- Remove internal detail, literal environment, and photographic shading.
- Convert the event into four to eight broad hand-cut shapes.
- Let one irregular negative gap carry the action; simplify faces unless identity is essential.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium` to `high`.
- Use extreme simplification and asymmetry, but retain the event.
- Keep one dominant warm or cool field and no more than four supporting colors.

## Color and material

Painted paper in ultramarine, vermilion, leaf green, ochre, black, and warm off-white; visible scissor wobble, matte gouache, and slight paper lift.

## Avoid

Copies of famous cut-outs, decorative leaf motifs unrelated to the source, children's craft look, perfect vector curves, rainbow confetti, thick outlines, gradients, facial invention, text, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The source action must remain readable from shape and spacing alone. If the result resembles a generic decorative cut-paper print, the style failed.
