# 瑞日留白编辑风

Internal ID: `swiss-japanese-editorial`

## North star

Use a disciplined Swiss-derived grid, Japanese editorial restraint, an asymmetric image crop, and one precise graphic accent. Treat quiet space and alignment as the material; do not imitate a magazine cover with invented copy.

## Best for

Architecture, objects, fashion, calm portraits, minimal travel frames, exhibitions, façades, windows, and still-life arrangements with one clear focal axis.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 白墙、门窗、立面、展馆和精确阴影天然适合网格与留白。
- `风景：中` — 极简海岸、单树、远山或安静旅行画面有明确主轴时适合。
- `人像：强` — 安静半身、服装肖像和克制姿势适合；混乱活动现场不适合。

## Preserve

- One clean hero crop or object axis.
- One real alignment, edge, shadow, or interval from the source.
- Up to three recognition anchors.

## Delete and transform

- Remove background noise and nonessential texture.
- Place one photographic or flattened image fragment on an asymmetric grid.
- Add one nonverbal rule, dot, block, or color bar as an alignment accent; default to no text.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `low` to `medium`.
- Keep 45–70% quiet paper and one deliberate edge tension.
- Do not fake editorial content. Add text only when the user supplies exact copy.

## Color and material

Soft white or warm grey paper, charcoal, one vermilion or cobalt accent, precise hairline rules, subtle print grain, and restrained photographic contrast.

## Avoid

Invented Japanese characters, fake issue numbers, lorem ipsum, oversized vertical text, generic Swiss poster clichés, rigid centered symmetry, UI-card layouts, decorative grids, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The crop, interval, and alignment must feel source-specific. If removing the image leaves a reusable template, the style failed.
