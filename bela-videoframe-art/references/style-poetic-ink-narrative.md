# 诗性水墨叙事

Internal ID: `poetic-ink-narrative`

## North star

Keep only the relationship that makes the frame meaningful, then express it with sparse ink marks, broken silhouettes, and generous paper.

## Best for

Walking, waiting, following, carrying, looking, a person with an animal or object, vehicles in motion, flocks, repeated gestures, and scenes with a clear emotional or directional relation.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：弱` — 建筑只宜保留为一条墙线、门洞或远景关系，不适合展示立面细节。
- `风景：中` — 雨、雾、水面、空地或远山能承托动作关系时适合。
- `人像：强` — 牵手、等待、跟随、搬运和人与动物关系比单人摆拍更适合。

## Preserve

- The event's action and direction.
- The relationship between the hero and one supporting subject or environment cue.
- Up to three silhouette anchors.

## Delete and transform

- Remove literal background description and most surface detail.
- Translate the hero into a partial silhouette or a few weighted brush marks.
- Use one sparse repeated motif only when it already exists in the source.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium` to `high`.
- Keep 55–80% quiet paper and place the relationship, not the object inventory, at the visual center.
- Reframe without inventing new narrative participants.

## Color and material

Warm ivory paper, charcoal, diluted blue-gray or umber ink, dry brush, pooled edges, occasional restrained reflection or shadow.

## Avoid

Generic East-Asian decoration, calligraphy text, red seals, random birds, fantasy fog, literal photo tracing, full-scene outlining, sentimental captions, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The viewer should recognize what is happening with very few marks. If the image needs detailed scenery to explain itself, the chosen frame or style is wrong.
