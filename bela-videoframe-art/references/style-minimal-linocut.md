# 极简木刻版画

Internal ID: `minimal-linocut`

## North star

Reduce the frame to one carved silhouette, one sparse gouge rhythm, and a hard two-tone value structure. The pressure and missing ink should feel hand-pulled rather than digitally distressed.

## Best for

Strong poses, animals, trees, tools, buildings, bicycles, vehicles, and objects that remain recognizable in black and paper white.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：中` — 单体建筑、塔、桥或屋顶轮廓鲜明时可用，细碎立面不适合。
- `风景：中` — 树、山、岩石等能压成一两块大剪影时可用。
- `人像：强` — 逆光、侧影、劳动动作和全身姿态比细腻表情更适合。

## Preserve

- The hero's outer silhouette and direction.
- Up to three identity or object anchors.
- One source-derived internal rhythm such as folds, fur direction, spokes, or grain.

## Delete and transform

- Remove gradients, photographic shading, small texture, and most background description.
- Convert one light-dark boundary into a carved field.
- Use a few tapered gouges to reveal paper; do not outline every object.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `low` to `medium`.
- Keep the hero large and crop only where the silhouette remains decisive.
- Use two dominant values and at most one small spot color.

## Color and material

Black or deep indigo ink, warm paper, optional oxblood or cobalt accent, dry roller gaps, uneven edge pressure, and visible carved direction.

## Avoid

Clip-art contour, coloring-book outlines, comic halftone, vector-perfect hatching, decorative woodgrain everywhere, fake lettering, extra anatomy, gradients, 3D shading, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The hero must read immediately as a carved value mass at phone size. If the effect depends on a generic distress texture, the style failed.
