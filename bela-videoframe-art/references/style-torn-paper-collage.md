# 撕纸解构拼贴

Internal ID: `torn-paper-collage`

## North star

Rebuild the source from a few deliberately torn image and paper planes. Let exposed fiber edges, interrupted scale, and partial overlap create the spatial tension; do not simulate a scrapbook full of decorations.

## Best for

Travel, architecture, people moving through space, roads, windows, layered foreground/background, and frames with at least two separable depth planes.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 层叠立面、门窗、巷道和室内外关系能自然拆成纸片层。
- `风景：强` — 山体、道路、岸线或前中后景边界清楚时最有效。
- `人像：中` — 适合人物置身空间或行走场景；不适合只靠五官成立的大头肖像。

## Preserve

- One hero silhouette or action.
- One source-derived environmental plane.
- Up to three recognition anchors that remain visible across the tears.

## Delete and transform

- Remove most photographic inventory and repeated foliage.
- Divide the scene into three to six large fragments; allow one purposeful scale break.
- Expose warm paper in the gaps and use one overlap to clarify, not hide, the event.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium` to `high`.
- Use one dominant torn boundary and one secondary overlap family.
- Never tear through a face, hand, culturally meaningful detail, or the only action cue.

## Color and material

Warm uncoated paper, one retained photographic fragment, one desaturated field, one dark ink anchor, visible deckled fiber, restrained paste shadow, and imperfect hand-cut alignment.

## Avoid

Scrapbook stickers, tape on every corner, fake newspaper text, ransom-note typography, dozens of tiny scraps, glossy drop shadows, stock ephemera, extra flowers or birds, invented labels, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The tear must reorganize space and hierarchy. If the image is only a photo with a ripped border, the style failed.
