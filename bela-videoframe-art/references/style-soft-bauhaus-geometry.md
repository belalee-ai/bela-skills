# 柔性包豪斯几何

Internal ID: `soft-bauhaus-geometry`

## North star

Translate the source into pale circles, arcs, bars, and one softened asymmetric grid. Geometry should reveal the source relationship, not turn the frame into a generic poster template.

## Best for

Furniture, façades, portraits with a simple pose, product-like objects, windows, wheels, shadows, repeated shapes, and clean architecture.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 圆窗、拱门、楼梯、阳台和重复立面可以直接转成几何语法。
- `风景：弱` — 只有地平线、太阳、道路或山坡能化成少量大几何时才适合。
- `人像：中` — 简单姿势、纯色服装或几何道具明确时成立，复杂群像慎选。

## Preserve

- One hero contour or object axis.
- One real circle, arc, bar, or grid relationship from the source.
- Up to three recognition anchors.

## Delete and transform

- Remove literal background detail and photographic texture.
- Convert source geometry into four to seven large shapes.
- Use overlap and cropped circles to imply depth; keep one irregular human edge.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium`.
- Use an asymmetric grid with one dominant circle or arc, not a centered icon stack.
- Keep 35–60% quiet paper so the geometry can breathe.

## Color and material

Chalk, sand, pale peach, dusty blue, sage, and one dark ink anchor; matte gouache, faint pencil registration, and soft paper tooth.

## Avoid

Primary-color Bauhaus cliché, perfect vector circles, generic Canva poster layout, centered symmetry, floating shape confetti, gradients, 3D spheres, decorative text, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

At least one geometric relationship must be visibly derived from the source. If the subject could be swapped without changing the poster, the style failed.
