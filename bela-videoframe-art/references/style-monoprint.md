# 单版压印绘画

Internal ID: `monoprint`

## North star

Express the frame as one pressure-printed silhouette with uneven ink transfer, a restrained ghost edge, and plate tone. The image should feel singular and physically pressed, not mass-produced.

## Best for

Portraits, hands, animals, plants, solitary objects, strong gestures, and close or medium frames with one decisive mass.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：弱` — 单一门洞、雕塑性建筑局部或巨大轮廓可用，复杂立面不适合。
- `风景：弱` — 单树、孤石或单一地平线可用，多层远景会削弱压印主体。
- `人像：强` — 单人近中景、手部劳动和人与物接触最能发挥墨色压力与擦拭高光。

## Preserve

- One hero silhouette and direction.
- Up to three identity or object anchors.
- One pressure edge or contact relationship from the source.

## Delete and transform

- Remove detailed background, gradients, and secondary objects.
- Convert the hero into one dominant ink mass with selectively wiped highlights.
- Allow one pale ghost offset only where it clarifies motion or pressure.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `low` to `medium`.
- Keep one main plate impression and generous quiet paper.
- Avoid multiple equal subjects unless their contact relationship is the event.

## Color and material

Carbon black, deep umber, indigo, or oxblood on warm rag paper; rolled plate tone, wiped light, pressure bloom, feathered edge, and one faint ghost pull.

## Avoid

Screenprint halftone, linocut gouges, many color layers, digital grunge overlays, symmetrical inkblots, decorative splatter, fake signatures, text, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

Uneven transfer and wiped light must describe the source form. If the output is simply a black silhouette with noise added, the style failed.
