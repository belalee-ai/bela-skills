# 大地色氛围绘画

Internal ID: `earthtone-atmospheric`

## North star

Let documentary structure emerge through dust, rubbed pigment, worn paper, and restrained edges rather than polished photographic detail.

## Best for

Old vehicles, wood, animals, rural streets, brick, stone, sand, weathered walls, overcast scenes, journeys, labor, and subjects whose material history matters.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 砖墙、木门、石屋、旧厂房和风化立面能保留材料时间感。
- `风景：强` — 土路、荒地、乡村街道、沙地和低饱和远景最适合。
- `人像：中` — 劳动者和环境人像适合；干净棚拍或精致肤色肖像不适合。

## Preserve

- The hero's silhouette, action, and direction.
- One material anchor such as wood, metal, cloth, brick, earth, or fur.
- The main ground plane and spatial relationship.

## Delete and transform

- Dissolve secondary edges and background objects into a shared tonal field.
- Keep only a few sharper anchors around the hero.
- Replace photographic microdetail with scumbled pigment, dry pastel, wax, or worn canvas texture.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `medium`.
- Preserve the event but allow edges to disappear into atmosphere.
- Maintain one quiet tonal path; do not add cinematic spectacle.

## Color and material

Raw umber, sand, bone, smoke gray, muted rust, faded ochre, matte pigment, erased passages, dry-brush drag, subtle paper or canvas grain.

## Avoid

Sepia filters, faux-vintage borders, orange-teal grading, romanticized poverty, invented cultural detail, excessive fog, glossy concept art, text, logos, signatures, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The image must feel materially painted and selectively remembered, not like a photograph with brown color grading.
