# 地理抽象拼贴

Internal ID: `cartographic-collage`

## North star

Turn a real route, shoreline, horizon, or movement trajectory into the image's organizing line, then rebuild the surrounding terrain from a few map-like paper fields.

## Best for

Roads, rivers, coastlines, parks, aerial views, hills, journeys, vehicles moving through terrain, and travel scenes with a readable origin-to-destination relationship.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：中` — 建筑群、街区和道路关系清楚时适合，单栋建筑近景不适合。
- `风景：强` — 河流、海岸、道路、山脊和航拍地形是最自然的素材。
- `人像：弱` — 人物通常只作为路线中的小比例尺，不适合强调肖像身份。

## Preserve

- One true route, boundary, or direction from the source.
- The hero as a small but traceable scale cue.
- One land/water, ground/sky, or near/far relationship.

## Delete and transform

- Remove detailed buildings, foliage, street furniture, and photographic texture.
- Convert terrain into three to six cut-paper regions and one route line.
- Use contour bands only where they echo real elevation or depth; do not invent a factual map.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `high`.
- State clearly that the result is an abstract spatial interpretation, not navigational cartography.
- Use one route line and one boundary family; avoid dense map notation.

## Color and material

Parchment, mineral blue, moss, clay, charcoal, translucent tracing paper, torn contour edges, and one stitched or drawn route line.

## Avoid

Invented place names, coordinates, borders, flags, territorial claims, fake legends, compass roses, location pins, dense labels, literal app-map UI, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The source's geography or movement must remain traceable without pretending to be an accurate map. If the route is arbitrary decoration, the style failed.
