# 日照留白摄影

Internal ID: `sunlit-negative-space`

## North star

Build a quiet image from one isolated subject, one architectural plane, and one decisive natural shadow. The empty field is the main compositional material.

## Best for

White or pale walls, windows, doors, plants, solitary objects, hard daylight, clean ground lines, and frames that can survive with most context removed.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 白墙、门窗、台阶、柱子和硬日光阴影是最佳组合。
- `风景：中` — 单树、孤岸、空旷地面或清楚地平线能留下大量安静空间时适合。
- `人像：弱` — 只适合人物很小的环境人像；不适合依靠脸部和身份细节的肖像。

## Preserve

- One hero silhouette or object.
- One supporting architectural cue such as a window, wall edge, ledge, or doorway.
- The real light direction when it is readable.

## Delete and transform

- Remove secondary objects, visual clutter, signage, foliage noise, and texture variation.
- Reduce the scene to a pale plane, one small color accent, and one long shadow.
- Scale the hero down when necessary so that empty space occupies roughly 65–85% of the image.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `high`.
- Use one off-center subject and one shadow vector.
- Prefer expansion or reconstruction of a plausible plain surface over stretching the original frame.

## Color and material

Warm white, chalk, pale stone, one muted blue/green/terracotta accent, natural sun, restrained photographic grain.

## Avoid

Lifestyle props, decorative clouds, luxury-resort clichés, fake Mediterranean location claims, symmetrical catalog staging, glossy HDR, text, logos, and extra objects.

## Quality gate

The image must feel quiet because of proportion and light, not because a blur or white filter was applied.
