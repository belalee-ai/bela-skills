# 当代线性构成

Internal ID: `linear-constructive`

## North star

Reduce the subject to a few assertive contours and turn one real repeated detail into a controlled field of hand-drawn lines.

## Best for

Portraits, clothing folds, striped fabric, machinery, wheels, grilles, fences, canopies, wood slats, architecture, and objects with a recognizable silhouette plus internal rhythm.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：强` — 栏杆、格栅、雨棚、百叶窗和重复立柱能直接成为线条系统。
- `风景：弱` — 只有田垄、树列、电线或道路形成真实重复节奏时才适合。
- `人像：强` — 条纹服装、褶皱、发型和清楚外轮廓最适合，纯脸部特写慎选。

## Preserve

- The outer contour and direction of the hero.
- Up to three identity or object anchors.
- One authentic repeated structure from the source.

## Delete and transform

- Remove photographic shading and small surface detail.
- Translate one repeated feature into uneven parallel, radial, or crosshatched lines.
- Flatten the remaining regions into two or three color fields.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `low` to `medium`.
- Keep the hero large enough for the line rhythm to read at phone size.
- Use one line system only; do not outline every object with equal emphasis.

## Color and material

Warm paper, black contour, one dominant ink color such as oxblood, cobalt, or forest green, with visible hand pressure and small inconsistencies.

## Avoid

Vector-perfect lines, coloring-book outlines, zentangle decoration, fake handwriting, extra facial features, random patterns, gradients, 3D shading, text, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

The line rhythm must come from the source and carry the composition. If removing the lines leaves a generic flat illustration, the style failed.
