# 色域地景绘画

Internal ID: `colorfield-landscape`

## North star

Reconstruct the scene as three to five large spatial fields, one dominant edge or curve, and a tiny but traceable scale cue.

## Best for

Roads, water, grass, walls, sky, slopes, fields, aerial views, long shadows, and scenes whose geography remains readable after most detail disappears.

## Subject fit

- `建筑：中` — 极简墙面、屋顶和建筑群能变成少量大色块时适合。
- `风景：强` — 河流、海面、草坡、麦田、天空和航拍边界最能发挥色域关系。
- `人像：弱` — 人物通常缩成一个尺度点，不适合强调面孔或服装细节。

## Preserve

- The dominant boundary or route.
- The hero's direction and one recognizable silhouette.
- The source's essential foreground/background relationship.

## Delete and transform

- Delete almost all texture, small foliage, mechanical detail, architecture detail, and secondary objects.
- Rebuild the scene as three to five matte color fields.
- Scale the hero down to a graphic cue when the landscape needs to dominate.

## Composition contract

- Default composition change: `high`.
- Use one sweeping edge, extreme scale contrast, or long-shadow rhythm; never combine all three.
- Tell the user explicitly when the hero will become much smaller.

## Color and material

One saturated field, one contrasting land or ground field, warm off-white, very dark accents, flat gouache, faint brush drag, matte paper.

## Avoid

Literal Deconstructivism claims, retained photographic detail, many tiny objects, decorative birds or trees, watercolor gradients, thick universal outlines, children's-book styling, text, logos, and watermark.

## Quality gate

At thumbnail size, the eye must read one field relationship before it reads the hero. The source geography must remain traceable.
